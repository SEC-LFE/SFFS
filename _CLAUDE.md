## Naming
- Dated notes: `YYYY-MM-DD — Title.md`
- Evergreen notes: `Title.md`
- No special characters except em dash (—)

## Frontmatter
Every note needs at minimum:
```yaml
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [note-type]
---
```

## Writing Style
- Use `[[wikilinks]]` for internal links
- AI-first: self-contained context, recency markers, sources verbatim
- Never append to bottom — edit specific sections
